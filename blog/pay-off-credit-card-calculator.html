How Long Will It Take to Pay Off My Credit Card? (With Calculator)

Published: January 26, 2026 • By CalcGen

If you only make minimum payments on your credit card, you could be paying for over a decade — and spending more on interest than the original balance. Here's exactly how long different payment strategies take.

Credit Card Payoff: Minimum vs Fixed Payments

Shocking truth: Minimum payments on $5,000 at 22% = over 15 years and $6,500+ in interest. You'd pay back $11,500+ on a $5,000 balance.

How to Pay Off Credit Card Debt Faster

  • Pay more than the minimum. Even $50 extra per month can cut years off your payoff time.
  • Use the avalanche method. Pay minimums on everything, then put all extra money toward the highest-rate card.
  • Consider a balance transfer. A 0% APR card can pause interest for 12-21 months. But pay it off before the promotional period ends.
  • Stop using the card. You can't climb out of a hole while still digging.
  • Negotiate a lower rate. Call your card issuer. If you've been a good customer, they may reduce your APR.

The Math Behind the Payoff Time

The formula: Months = -log(1 - r×B/P) / log(1 + r), where r = monthly rate, B = balance, P = payment. Or just use our loan calculator — set the loan term high and see how payments affect payoff time.
