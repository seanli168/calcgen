Metric vs Imperial Units: A Practical Conversion Guide

Published: January 22, 2026 • By CalcGen

Only three countries — the US, Liberia, and Myanmar — still primarily use the imperial system. The rest of the world uses metric. If you travel, cook international recipes, or read scientific papers, you need to convert between them. Here's how.

Length: Miles to Kilometers

1 mile = 1.60934 km | Quick mental math: miles × 1.6 ≈ km

10 miles ≈ 16.1 km. A marathon (26.2 miles) = 42.2 km.

Weight: Pounds to Kilograms

1 pound = 0.4536 kg | Quick: pounds ÷ 2.2 ≈ kg

150 lbs ≈ 68 kg. A kilogram is about 2.2 pounds.

Temperature: Fahrenheit to Celsius

°C = (°F - 32) × 5/9 | Quick: subtract 30, then halve

68°F = 20°C (room temperature). 98.6°F = 37°C (body temperature).
