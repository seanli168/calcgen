What Is a Good BMI for My Height? Healthy Weight Range Chart

Published: January 24, 2026 • By CalcGen

A "good" BMI is 18.5 to 24.9 — that's the healthy range for all adults. But the weight that corresponds to that range depends entirely on your height. Here's exactly what a healthy weight looks like for heights from 5'0" to 6'4".

How to Use This Chart

Find your height, then check if your weight falls within the healthy range. If not, don't stress — BMI has limitations. Athletes with high muscle mass, for example, may have a BMI above 25 while being very healthy. Use our BMI calculator for your exact number.

BMI for Men vs Women

The BMI formula and healthy range are identical for men and women: 18.5–24.9. However, women naturally carry 6-11% more body fat than men. So at the same BMI, a woman typically has a higher body fat percentage. That's why body fat measurement is often more useful than BMI alone.
