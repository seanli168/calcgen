How Many Calories Should I Eat to Lose Weight?

Published: January 24, 2026 • By CalcGen

It's the most Googled weight loss question: "How many calories should I eat to lose weight?" The answer depends on your body, your activity level, and how fast you want results. Here's your complete guide.

Quick Answer: Calorie Targets by Person

Sedentary woman, 5'4", 165 lbs~1,900~1,400
Moderate woman, 5'6", 150 lbs~2,200~1,700

How to Find Your Personal Calorie Target

  1. Calculate your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) using our calorie calculator.
  2. Subtract 300-500 calories for weight loss. A 500-calorie deficit = ~1 lb fat loss per week.
  3. Never go below 1,200 (women) or 1,500 (men) without medical supervision.

Example: 5'6" woman, 150 lbs, exercises 3x/week. Maintenance = 2,200 cal. Weight loss target = 1,700 cal/day. Expected loss: ~1 lb/week.

What Does 1,500 Calories Look Like?

Breakfast (400 cal): 2 eggs + 1 slice whole wheat toast + 1/2 avocado
Lunch (450 cal): 4 oz grilled chicken + 2 cups mixed greens + 2 tbsp dressing
Dinner (500 cal): 4 oz salmon + 1 cup quinoa + roasted vegetables
Snack (150 cal): Greek yogurt + berries

Quality Matters More Than Quantity

1,500 calories of junk food leaves you hungry and undernourished. 1,500 calories of protein, vegetables, and healthy fats keeps you full and preserves muscle. Use our macro calculator to get the right balance of protein, carbs, and fats within your calorie target.
