How Much Should I Walk to Lose Weight? 4-Week Plan

Published: January 27, 2026 • By CalcGen

Walking is the most underrated weight loss tool. It's free, low-impact, and you can do it anywhere. This plan shows you exactly how much to walk each week — and how to combine it with a calorie deficit for real results.

Walking Calorie Burn: What to Expect

  • Brisk walking (3.5 mph): ~100 calories per mile for a 180-lb person; ~65 calories per mile for a 120-lb person.
  • 30 minutes brisk walking: ~150-200 calories
  • 60 minutes brisk walking: ~300-400 calories

4-Week Walking for Weight Loss Plan

Week 1: Build the Habit

Goal: 7,000 steps/day. Walk 20-30 minutes daily at a comfortable pace. No need to push — just build consistency. Expected calorie burn: 100-150 per walk.

Week 2: Increase Duration

Goal: 8,000-10,000 steps/day. Walk 30-40 minutes daily. Add one longer walk (45-60 min) on a weekend day. Expected calorie burn: 200-300 per walk.

Week 3: Add Intensity

Goal: 10,000-12,000 steps/day. Include intervals: walk briskly for 3 minutes, then at a moderate pace for 2 minutes. Repeat 6-8 times. Expected calorie burn: 300-400 per walk.

Week 4: Maximize Results

Goal: 12,000-15,000 steps/day. Two shorter walks (morning + evening) often work better than one long walk. Add incline or hills when possible. Expected calorie burn: 400-600 per day total.

Walking + Calorie Deficit = The Formula

Diet: 300-calorie daily deficit through food choices.
Walking: 300 calories burned through 60 minutes of walking.
Total daily deficit: 600 calories × 7 days = 4,200 calories = ~1.2 lbs fat loss per week.

Use our calorie calculator to find your maintenance calories, then subtract 300. Let walking handle the rest.

Tips to Walk More Every Day

  • Park at the far end of the parking lot
  • Take walking meetings or phone calls
  • Walk while watching TV (march in place or use a walking pad)
  • Take the stairs instead of elevator
  • Set an hourly reminder to stand up and walk 250 steps
