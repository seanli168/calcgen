How Many Steps a Day to Lose Weight? Science-Backed Guide

Published: January 26, 2026 • By CalcGen

10,000 steps is the number everyone talks about. But is it really enough to lose weight? And how many calories do steps actually burn? Here's what the research says.

How Many Calories Do Steps Burn?

Calories burned per step depends on your weight and pace:

  • 120 lbs: ~0.03 calories/step
  • 150 lbs: ~0.04 calories/step
  • 180 lbs: ~0.05 calories/step
  • 210 lbs: ~0.06 calories/step

10,000 steps at 180 lbs: ~500 calories burned. That's about one meal's worth of calories.

Step Goals for Weight Loss

Current level: Sedentary (3,000-5,000 steps/day). Start with 7,000-8,000 steps. Each 2,000 extra steps = ~80-120 calories burned.

  • Maintenance: 7,000-8,000 steps/day
  • Mild weight loss: 10,000-12,000 steps/day
  • Significant weight loss: 12,000-15,000 steps/day

Walking + Calorie Deficit = The Winning Formula

Walking alone won't cause weight loss if you eat back the calories. The best approach: use a calorie deficit as your primary tool, and use walking to increase the deficit.

Example plan: Calorie deficit of 300 cal from diet + 200 cal burned from walking = 500 cal total daily deficit = ~1 lb fat loss per week. Use our calorie calculator to find your target.
