BMI Calculator for Women Over 40: What's a Healthy Weight?

Published: January 25, 2026 • By CalcGen

Turning 40 changes your body — even if your weight stays the same. Muscle mass decreases, fat distribution shifts, and hormones change. Here's what women over 40 need to know about BMI and healthy weight.

Healthy BMI for Women Over 40

The standard healthy BMI range is 18.5–24.9 for all adult women. However, many doctors suggest women over 40 aim for a BMI of 22–27 rather than the lower end. Why? Postmenopausal women with very low BMI have higher risk of osteoporosis. A BMI of 23–25 may be "sweet spot" for bone health while avoiding obesity-related risks.

Important: BMI is just one number. Waist circumference matters more after 40. Women with waist > 35 inches have higher health risks even with normal BMI.

Why BMI Alone Isn't Enough After 40

  • Muscle loss: Women lose 3-5% of muscle mass per decade after 30. Same weight, same BMI — but higher body fat percentage.
  • Fat redistribution: Estrogen decline shifts fat storage from hips/thighs to abdomen. Belly fat is more dangerous than lower-body fat.
  • Metabolism slows: TDEE decreases ~100-200 calories/day per decade. Eating the same as at 30 leads to weight gain.

Healthy Weight Ranges for Women by Height

For BMI 22–27 (often optimal for women over 40):

  • 5'2" (157 cm): 120–148 lbs
  • 5'4" (163 cm): 128–157 lbs
  • 5'5" (165 cm): 132–162 lbs
  • 5'6" (168 cm): 137–168 lbs
  • 5'8" (173 cm): 145–178 lbs

Better Measures Than BMI Alone

  • Waist circumference: Keep under 35 inches for women.
  • Waist-to-hip ratio: Under 0.85 is healthy.
  • Body fat percentage: 25-33% is normal for women 40-60.
  • Strength & energy levels: How you feel matters more than a number.
