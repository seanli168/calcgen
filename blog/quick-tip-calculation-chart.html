Quick Mental Math Trick

To calculate 20% tip instantly: move the decimal one place left (10%), then double it. Example: $45 → $4.50 → $9.00. For 15%: take 10%, then add half of that. Example: $60 → $6 + $3 = $9.00.

How Much Should You Tip?

  • Restaurant (full service): 15-20% of pre-tax
  • Quick service / buffet: 10%
  • Delivery: $3-5 or 10-15%
  • Bartender: $1-2 per drink or 15-20%
