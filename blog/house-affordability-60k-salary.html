How Much House Can I Afford with a $60,000 Salary?

Published: January 27, 2026 • By CalcGen

A $60,000 salary means your monthly gross income is $5,000. Using the 28/36 rule, here's exactly what that means for your home buying budget — with real payment numbers.

The Quick Answer

With 20% down: Max home price ~$200,000-$220,000. Monthly payment ~$1,400 (including tax & insurance). With 5% down: Max home price ~$170,000-$185,000 (PMI reduces your buying power).

How Debt Affects Your Budget

The 28/36 rule says total debts (housing + car + student loans + credit cards) should stay under 36% of income = $1,800/month total. If you have a $400 car payment and $200 in student loans, your max housing drops to $1,200/month — and your max home price drops by $30,000-$40,000.

Tips for Buying on a $60K Salary

  • Save aggressively for a down payment. 20% eliminates PMI and gives you more house.
  • Pay down existing debt first. It directly increases your buying power.
  • Consider first-time buyer programs. FHA loans require only 3.5% down but have permanent mortgage insurance.
  • Shop interest rates. Even a 0.5% lower rate adds $15,000-$20,000 to your max home price.
