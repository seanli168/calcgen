How to Calculate 20% Off (The Simple Way)

Method 1: Multiply the price by 0.20 to get the discount. Subtract from original. Example: $80 × 0.20 = $16 off. $80 - $16 = $64.

Method 2 (Quicker): Multiply the price by 0.80 to get the sale price directly. Example: $80 × 0.80 = $64.

What About 20% Off + an Extra 10%?

Stores often stack discounts — but they don't add them. 20% off + extra 10% off ≠ 30% off. The second discount applies to the already-reduced price. Example: $100 → 20% off = $80 → extra 10% off = $72 (total 28% off). Use our sale price calculator to handle stacked discounts.
