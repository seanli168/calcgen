How to Save $10,000 in a Year: Month-by-Month Plan

Published: January 27, 2026 • By CalcGen

Saving $10,000 in 12 months sounds daunting. But broken down, it's $834 per month or about $28 per day. With the right plan, it's more achievable than you think.

The Math: How to Hit $10,000

$10,000 ÷ 12 months = $834/month
$10,000 ÷ 52 weeks = $193/week
$10,000 ÷ 365 days = $27.40/day

Month-by-Month Savings Plan

  1. Month 1: Audit all subscriptions and expenses. Cancel unused ones. Goal: $500.
  2. Month 2: Switch to a budget grocery plan. Save $200-300 vs eating out. Goal: $800.
  3. Month 3: Sell unused items (clothes, electronics, furniture). Goal: $1,000.
  4. Month 4: Reduce utility bills (lower thermostat, energy-efficient habits). Goal: $800.
  5. Month 5: Pick up a side hustle (freelancing, delivery, tutoring). Goal: $1,000.
  6. Month 6: Mid-year bonus or tax refund? Save 100% of it. Goal: $1,200.
  7. Months 7-12: Maintain habits. Automate $834/month transfer to savings account. Goal: $5,000.

Total: $10,300 — exceeding the goal with a small buffer.

Where to Find $834 Per Month

  • Skip daily coffee shop visit: $150/month
  • Cook 10 more meals at home: $250/month
  • Cancel 2-3 unused subscriptions: $60/month
  • Negotiate bills (phone, internet, insurance): $75/month
  • Side hustle (5 hours/week at $15/hr): $300/month

Total found: $835/month — without a major lifestyle sacrifice.

Automate It — The Most Important Step

Set up an automatic transfer of $200 every week from checking to a high-yield savings account. If you never see the money, you won't spend it. At 4% APY, your $10,000 earns an extra $400 in interest over the year.
