Color Theory Basics: Understanding HEX, RGB & HSL

Published: January 22, 2026 • By CalcGen

Every color on a screen is defined by a code. Understanding HEX, RGB, and HSL gives you complete control over your designs. This guide covers the three main color formats and how to use them.

HEX Codes

HEX codes are 6-digit numbers like #2563EB. Each pair represents red, green, and blue in hexadecimal (00-FF). #FF0000 = pure red, #00FF00 = pure green, #0000FF = pure blue, #000000 = black, #FFFFFF = white.

RGB Colors

RGB uses three numbers from 0-255: rgb(37, 99, 235). Same concept as HEX, different notation. Useful in CSS and graphic design software.

HSL Colors

HSL = Hue (0-360° on color wheel), Saturation (0-100%), Lightness (0-100%). hsl(221, 83%, 53%). HSL is often more intuitive for designers because you adjust one property at a time.
