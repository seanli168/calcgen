How to Calculate Overtime Pay: A Complete Guide

Published: January 23, 2026 • By CalcGen

Under US federal law (FLSA), overtime pay is 1.5x your regular hourly rate for any hours worked beyond 40 in a workweek. Some states have additional rules — California requires overtime for hours over 8 in a single day. Here's how to calculate it.

Time-and-a-Half Formula

Overtime Pay = Regular Rate × 1.5 × Overtime Hours
Example: $20/hr × 1.5 × 5 OT hours = $150 overtime

Step-by-Step Calculation

  • Step 1: Calculate regular pay: Regular Rate × 40 hours
  • Step 2: Calculate overtime pay: Regular Rate × 1.5 × (Total Hours - 40)
  • Step 3: Total pay = Regular Pay + Overtime Pay

Example: $20/hr, worked 47 hours. Regular = $20 × 40 = $800. Overtime = $30 × 7 = $210. Total = $1,010.

Double Time

Some employers or states require double time (2x) for extreme hours. In California, hours over 12 in a day or over 8 on the 7th consecutive day are paid at double time.
