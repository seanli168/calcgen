What Is a Good Interest Rate on a Car Loan? By Credit Score (2026)

Published: January 26, 2026 • By CalcGen

A "good" car loan rate depends entirely on your credit score. Here's what you should expect to pay in 2026, based on real market data.

Average Car Loan Rates by Credit Score

Credit ScoreNew Car RateUsed Car Rate

Below 600 (Subprime)17.0%+20.0%+

Rates are estimates for 60-month loans. Actual rates vary by lender and market.

How Your Rate Affects Your Payment

On a $30,000 car loan for 60 months:

  • At 6%: $580/month, $4,800 total interest
  • At 10%: $637/month, $8,245 total interest
  • At 15%: $714/month, $12,826 total interest

Improving your credit from 650 to 720 could save you $4,000+ in interest on a single car loan. Use our loan calculator to compare rates.

How to Get the Best Car Loan Rate

  • Check your credit first. Know your score before you walk into a dealership.
  • Shop multiple lenders. Banks, credit unions, and online lenders — get 3-5 quotes.
  • Get pre-approved. A pre-approval gives you negotiating power at the dealership.
  • Consider a shorter term. 36-48 month loans usually have lower rates than 60-72 months.
  • Make a larger down payment. Lenders offer better rates when you have more skin in the game.
