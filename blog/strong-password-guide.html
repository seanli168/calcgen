How to Create a Strong Password: The Ultimate Guide

Published: January 22, 2026 • By CalcGen

Weak passwords are the #1 cause of account breaches. "password123" and "qwerty" are cracked in seconds. This guide shows you exactly how to create passwords that are virtually uncrackable — and how to manage them without losing your mind.

What Makes a Password Strong?

A strong password has four characteristics:

  • Length: At least 12 characters (16+ for important accounts)
  • Complexity: Mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
  • Randomness: Not based on dictionary words or personal information
  • Uniqueness: Never reused across multiple accounts

A 12-character random password with all character types takes a computer about 34,000 years to crack. An 8-character simple password takes about 5 minutes.

Password Don'ts

  • Don't use personal info (birthdays, pet names, addresses)
  • Don't use common words or keyboard patterns (qwerty, 123456)
  • Don't reuse passwords across sites
  • Don't share passwords via email or text

Use a Password Manager

The average person has 100+ online accounts. Remembering a unique strong password for each is impossible. Password managers (like Bitwarden, 1Password, or LastPass) generate and store strong passwords — you only need to remember one master password.
