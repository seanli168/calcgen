Free Retirement Calculator: Find Exactly How Much You Need to Retire

Published: January 25, 2026 • By CalcGen

Most Americans haven't calculated how much they need to retire. But it's the single most important number in your financial life. Our free retirement calculator takes 30 seconds and tells you exactly where you stand.

How the Calculator Works

It uses three numbers you already know: your current age, current savings, and monthly contributions. Then it projects your savings using compound interest and compares it to your retirement target using the 4% rule.

Example: Age 35, $50,000 saved, $800/month contribution, 7% return, retire at 65. Projected savings: ~$1.2 million. If you need $60,000/year in retirement, target = $1.5 million. Gap = $300,000 — the calculator shows you need ~$1,150/month to close it.

What the Calculator Shows You

  • Projected savings at retirement: What you'll have based on current contributions.
  • Nest egg target: How much you actually need (desired income × 25).
  • Savings gap: The difference. Green = on track. Yellow/Red = need to adjust.
  • Monthly contribution needed: Exactly how much more to save to close the gap.

Quick Retirement Targets by Income

  • $40,000/year desired: Save $1,000,000
  • $50,000/year desired: Save $1,250,000
  • $60,000/year desired: Save $1,500,000
  • $80,000/year desired: Save $2,000,000

Subtract Social Security from desired income first. If Social Security covers $20,000/year, your savings only need to cover the rest.
