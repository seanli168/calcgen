What Is a Good APR for a Personal Loan? By Credit Score

Published: January 27, 2026 • By CalcGen

Personal loan APRs range from 6% to 36%. Whether a rate is "good" depends entirely on your credit score. Here's what to expect and how to know if you're getting a fair deal.

Personal Loan APR by Credit Score

Credit ScoreRatingTypical APR Range
720+Excellent7% – 13%
690-719Good13% – 18%
630-689Fair18% – 25%
Below 630Poor25% – 36%

How APR Affects Your Payment

On a $10,000 personal loan for 3 years:

  • At 10%: $323/month, $1,616 total interest
  • At 18%: $361/month, $3,007 total interest
  • At 28%: $409/month, $4,727 total interest

A 10% vs 28% rate on the same $10,000 loan = $3,100 more in interest. That's why your credit score matters so much.

How to Get a Better Personal Loan Rate

  • Improve your credit score before applying. Even 20 points can mean a better rate.
  • Shop at least 3 lenders. Rates vary significantly between banks, credit unions, and online lenders.
  • Consider a secured loan. Using collateral (like a car) can lower your rate by 3-5%.
  • Add a co-signer. A co-signer with excellent credit can qualify you for prime rates.
