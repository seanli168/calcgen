How Many Words Should a Blog Post Be for SEO?

Published: January 22, 2026 • By CalcGen

The short answer: 1,500-2,500 words tends to rank best in Google. But word count alone doesn't guarantee rankings — quality, comprehensiveness, and user intent matter more. Here's what the data shows and how to find the right length for your content.

What the Research Says

Multiple studies of top-ranking pages find the average first-page result is about 1,400-1,900 words. But there's huge variation — some queries are answered perfectly in 300 words; others need 5,000+.

Key insight: Don't write to a word count. Write until you've thoroughly covered the topic. Use our word counter to track as you go.

Word Count by Content Type

  • How-to guides: 1,500-3,000 words
  • Listicles: 1,000-2,000 words
  • News articles: 300-800 words
  • Pillar pages: 2,500-5,000+ words
