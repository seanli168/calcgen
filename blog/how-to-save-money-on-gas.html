How to Save Money on Gas: 10 Tips That Actually Work

Published: January 23, 2026 • By CalcGen

Gas prices keep climbing, but your fuel costs don't have to. These 10 proven tips can cut your gas spending by 10-30% — without buying a new car.

10 Ways to Save on Gas

  1. Drive the speed limit. MPG drops sharply above 65 MPH. Every 5 MPH over 60 costs you about $0.20/gallon extra.
  2. Use cruise control. On highways, steady speed = better MPG.
  3. Keep tires inflated. Under-inflated tires increase rolling resistance. Check pressure monthly.
  4. Remove excess weight. An extra 100 lbs in your car reduces MPG by ~1%.
  5. Combine errands. A warm engine is more efficient than multiple cold starts.
  6. Use apps to find cheap gas. GasBuddy and similar apps show real-time prices near you.
  7. Don't idle. Idling for 10+ seconds uses more fuel than restarting.
  8. Change air filters. A clogged filter can reduce MPG by 10%.
  9. Use the right oil grade. Check your manual — the wrong oil can reduce MPG.
  10. Plan routes with fewer stops. Use GPS to avoid traffic and minimize stop-and-go.
