The Power of Starting Small

$100/month at 12% for 25 years = $189,764 — on just $30,000 invested. The remaining $159,764 is pure growth from compounding. Starting with $500/month = $948,820.

Key insight: Every 5 years of delay costs you enormously. $500/month starting at 25 vs 35 (25 years vs 15 years): $948,820 vs $252,290. A 10-year head start = $696,530 more.
