Frequently Asked Questions

What are macros?
Macronutrients — protein, carbs, and fats. Protein = 4 cal/g, Carbs = 4 cal/g, Fat = 9 cal/g.
What's the best macro split?
For fat loss: 35-40% protein. For muscle gain: 30% protein, higher carbs. For maintenance: balanced 30/40/30.
