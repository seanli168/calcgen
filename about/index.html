About CalcGen

CalcGen is a collection of free online calculators designed to help you make smarter financial decisions. No sign-ups, no fees — just simple tools that give you answers when you need them.

Our Mission

We believe financial tools should be accessible to everyone. Whether you're planning for retirement, saving for a home, or just curious about how compound interest works — our calculators are here to help.

Why CalcGen?

Most financial calculator sites are cluttered with ads, confusing interfaces, and outdated designs. We built CalcGen to be clean, fast, and easy to use on any device. Every tool loads instantly and works right in your browser — no downloads required.

Our Tools

We currently offer three free calculators, with more on the way:

  • Compound Interest Calculator — See how your savings grow over time
  • SIP Calculator — Calculate returns from monthly investments
  • Mortgage Calculator — Estimate your total monthly house payment
