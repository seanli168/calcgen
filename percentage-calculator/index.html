How to Use This Percentage Calculator

Choose a calculation mode using the tabs above:

  • % of a Number: What is X% of Y? (e.g., 20% of 150 = 30)

  • % to Decimal: Convert a percentage to a decimal instantly.

Percentage Formulas

Percent of a Number: Result = Number × (Percentage ÷ 100)

Percentage Change: Change = ((New - Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Find the Whole: Whole = Part ÷ (Percentage ÷ 100)

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a percentage?
Multiply the number by the percentage as a decimal. For example, 20% of 150 = 150 × 0.20 = 30.
How do you calculate percentage change?
((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100. Going from 100 to 125 is a 25% increase.
