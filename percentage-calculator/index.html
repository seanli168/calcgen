Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a percentage?
Multiply the number by the percentage as a decimal. 20% of 150 = 150 × 0.20 = 30.
How do you calculate percentage change?
((New - Old) / Old) × 100. 100 to 125 = 25% increase.
