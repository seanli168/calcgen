How to Use This Retirement Calculator

  • Current Age & Retirement Age: Sets your investing timeline.
  • Current Savings: Include all retirement accounts — 401(k), IRA, pension.
  • Monthly Contribution: Include employer match if applicable.
  • Expected Return: 6-8% for balanced portfolios; 10-12% for aggressive.
  • Desired Income: Aim for 70-80% of current income. Subtract Social Security/pension first.

How the 4% Rule Determines Your Retirement Number

This retirement savings calculator uses the 4% rule: multiply your desired annual income by 25. Need $60,000/year? Save $1.5 million. The rule works because withdrawing 4% annually (adjusted for inflation) has a 95%+ success rate over 30-year retirements. Learn more in our 4% rule guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do I need to retire?
Multiply your desired annual spending by 25 (4% rule). $60,000/year = $1.5 million target. Subtract Social Security and pension income first.
What is the 4% rule?
Withdraw 4% in year one of retirement, then adjust for inflation. Has 95% success rate over 30 years. Read our 4% rule guide.
