How to Use This Mortgage Calculator

Our mortgage calculator with taxes and insurance gives you a complete picture of your monthly housing costs — not just the principal and interest. Whether you're a first-time home buyer or refinancing an existing loan, this tool helps you understand exactly what you'll pay each month.

  • Home Price: The purchase price of the property.

How to Lower Your Mortgage Payment

  1. Save for a 20% down payment. This eliminates PMI and can save $100-$300/month.
  2. Improve your credit score. A 740+ score qualifies for the best mortgage rates — read our credit score guide.
  3. Shop multiple lenders. Rates vary. Getting 3-5 quotes can save thousands.
  4. Consider paying points. Paying 1% of the loan upfront can reduce your rate by 0.25%.
  5. Look at different loan terms. A 30-year mortgage has lower payments than 15-year (but more total interest).

Frequently Asked Questions About Mortgages

How is a mortgage payment calculated?
The monthly mortgage payment uses the amortization formula: M = P[r(1+r)^n]/[(1+r)^n-1]. P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is total payments. Our mortgage calculator with taxes adds property tax, insurance, PMI, and HOA on top.
What is PMI and when can I remove it?
PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) protects the lender when your down payment is under 20%. It costs 0.5-2% of the loan annually. You can request PMI cancellation once you reach 20% equity through payments or home appreciation. Use our calculator to see how much PMI adds to your payment.
How much house can I afford?
The 28/36 rule: spend under 28% of gross income on housing (PITI) and under 36% on total debt. With $6,000/month income, your mortgage payment should stay under $1,680. Read our guide to home affordability for a complete breakdown.
What's the difference between interest rate and APR?
The interest rate is the cost of borrowing the principal. APR (Annual Percentage Rate) includes the interest rate plus lender fees, points, and other charges — giving a truer picture of total borrowing cost. Always compare APRs when shopping for a mortgage.
Should I choose a 30-year or 15-year mortgage?
Choose a 30-year mortgage for lower monthly payments and more budget flexibility. Choose 15-year for much lower total interest (saving ~$200,000 on a typical loan) if you can afford the higher payments. Many buyers start with 30-year and refinance later.
How do property taxes affect my mortgage payment?
Property taxes are collected monthly by your lender as part of your escrow payment, then paid annually to your local government. They vary widely — from 0.5% to 2.5% of home value per year depending on location. Use our advanced options to enter your area's rate.
