Standard Tipping Guide

  • Restaurant (full service): 15-20%
  • Delivery: 10-15% or $3-5 minimum
  • Bartender: $1-2 per drink or 15-20%
  • Taxi/Rideshare: 10-15%
  • Haircut/Salon: 15-20%

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip at a restaurant?
In the US and Canada, the standard is 15-20% of the pre-tax bill. For exceptional service, 20-25%.
Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?
Most etiquette guides recommend tipping on the pre-tax subtotal. Either approach is acceptable.
How do I split a bill with friends?
Divide the total (including tip) by the number of people. This calculator does it automatically.
Is this tip calculator free?
Yes, completely free. No sign-up required.
