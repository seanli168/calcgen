Frequently Asked Questions

How is the monthly payment calculated?
M = P[r(1+r)^n]/[(1+r)^n-1] where P=loan amount, r=monthly rate, n=total payments.
APR vs interest rate?
APR includes fees; interest rate is just the borrowing cost. Always compare APRs.
Is this loan calculator free?
Yes, completely free for auto, personal, student, and other loan types.
