How to Use This SIP Calculator

  • Monthly Investment: How much you invest each month. Even $100/month adds up significantly.
  • Expected Return: Equity mutual funds historically return 10-12% long-term. Use 8-10% for conservative estimates.
  • Investment Period: Longer periods dramatically increase returns through compounding.
  • Initial Lump Sum: Optional one-time investment that also compounds.

SIP vs Lump Sum — Which Is Better?

$60,000 invested over 10 years at 12%: Lump sum = ~$186,350. SIP ($500/month) = ~$116,170. Lump sum wins mathematically, but SIP reduces the risk of investing right before a market crash. Read our complete SIP vs Lump Sum comparison.

SIP Calculation Formula

FV = P × ((1 + r)n - 1) / r × (1 + r)

Where P = monthly investment, r = monthly rate, n = total months. The (1+r) multiplier accounts for investments made at the start of each month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan invests a fixed amount regularly into mutual funds, using dollar-cost averaging to reduce timing risk. Start as low as $50/month. Read our SIP vs Lump Sum guide.
What's a good expected return rate?
10-12% for equity funds long-term. Use 8-10% for conservative planning and 5-7% for debt funds.
Can I start SIP with a small amount?
Yes, many funds start at $50/month. $100/month at 12% for 20 years = ~$99,000.
