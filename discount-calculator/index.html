Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off?
Multiply the original price by 0.20 to get the discount amount. Subtract from the original. For example, 20% off $85 = $17 off, final price $68.
How do I find the discount percentage?
Savings ÷ Original Price × 100. If $85 is on sale for $68, savings = $17, discount = ($17 ÷ $85) × 100 = 20%.
