Common Speed Conversions

MPH to KPH: Multiply by 1.60934. 60 MPH = 96.6 KPH.

Knots to MPH: Multiply by 1.15078. 10 knots = 11.5 MPH.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert MPH to KPH?
MPH × 1.60934 = KPH. Or use the converter above.
What is a knot?
One nautical mile per hour. 1 knot = 1.15 MPH = 1.85 KPH.
