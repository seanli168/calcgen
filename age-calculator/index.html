How to Use This Age Calculator

  • Enter your birth date in the date picker

  • Click Calculate to see your exact age

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated?
By subtracting your birth date from the current date (or your chosen end date), accounting for leap years and month lengths.
Does it account for leap years?
Yes. The calculator automatically accounts for leap years.
Is this calculator free?
Yes, completely free. No sign-up required.
