Frequently Asked Questions

How is GPA calculated?
GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credits. Each grade has point value (A=4.0). Multiply by credits, sum, divide by total credits.
What is a good GPA?
3.0+ is good. 3.5+ is competitive for grad school. 3.7+ is excellent.
